Why work with me

I do my best work side by side with product and engineering, not behind a handoff wall. I balance business goals with real user needs, and I care just as much about measurable results as I do about the small visual details that make a product feel crafted.

Away from the screen, I recharge in the kitchen and the garden. Cooking, watching something I planted grow, and dancing whenever the music is right keep my creative spark alive.
