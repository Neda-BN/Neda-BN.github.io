InventoryPro — App Icon & Launch Screen

Internal B2B tool at Focalpay

An app icon and launch screen for Focalpay's internal inventory management tool. B2B tools often get the least design attention — they're assumed to be for "power users" who don't care how things look. That assumption is wrong, and this project pushed back against it. The warehouse trolley icon was chosen for immediate recognisability: anyone who works with inventory knows what it means within a fraction of a second.

The paired filled/outlined icon treatment creates a consistent visual language with other Focalpay app icons, reinforcing the product family feel. The soft blue-gray palette keeps it neutral and professional — the right tone for a tool that warehouse teams will open dozens of times a day. Even for something as small as an app icon, legibility at every size was part of the process.
